Yi Ting Accounting Firm has been committed to providing professional financial and tax advisory services since its founding. We're pleased to announce that we've formally partnered with Japan's Pear Capital Co., Ltd. ([株式会社ペアキャピタル](https://p-capital.co.jp/)) to jointly develop cross-border M&A business between Taiwan and Japan.

![Taiwan-Japan cross-border M&A partnership](/uploads/workwithpearcapital/announcement.png)

This partnership marks a new milestone in the Taiwan–Japan M&A market. Together, we'll work to promote business exchange and international growth between the two countries. Whether you're a Taiwanese company looking for acquisition opportunities or a Japanese company looking to enter the Taiwan market, this partnership gives you tailored solutions and professional M&A support.

We believe this collaboration will create greater value for our clients and contribute to the shared growth of both economies. Together, Yi Ting Accounting Firm and Pear Capital will bring more innovation and opportunity to Taiwan–Japan cross-border M&A in the years ahead.

We look forward to partnering with more companies to explore opportunities in international markets.
