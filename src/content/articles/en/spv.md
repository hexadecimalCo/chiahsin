As mentioned earlier, renewable energy generation equipment can be applied for by individuals, companies, or organizations, as long as the installed capacity is under 2,000 kW.

Unless the power generated is entirely for self-use, if the electricity is sold to Taipower, it falls within the scope of business tax — and the business registration requirements, whether the seller can be exempt from issuing invoices, and how income tax is filed and paid all vary depending on the seller's status.
